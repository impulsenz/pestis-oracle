Critical limits

For flying insect control and crawling insect monitoring. Each site has its own limits: give a limit a name, then list the pests it watches and how many captures a month each station is allowed. Insect stations are put on a limit when they're set up (or tick them here); each station counts its own catches against the limit - e.g. with Midges 150, any one station that catches more than 150 midges in a month has breached. When a station goes over, the technician is asked to add a recommendation (they can skip it), the client is alerted for that station and it's highlighted on the service report.

Site contacts

The people at each site and their roles. The main contact shows on the site page in the Service App with their phone number. Technicians and the site's clients (in the Client Portal) can keep these up to date too.

Lists used in the station form
